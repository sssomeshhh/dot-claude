# CLAUDE.md

## Working principles

- **Fact-based work is a first-class requirement, with stringent traceability.** Every factual claim in deliverables (documents, proposals, reports, analyses) must be traceable to a source — cite exact quotes and URLs. Clearly separate verified facts from inferences/assumptions, and never present an unverified inference as fact. Verify load-bearing facts adversarially before relying on them; hedge or flag anything unconfirmed.
